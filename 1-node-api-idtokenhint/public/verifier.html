You'll need Microsoft Authenticator with your Verified Employee credential. Authenticator will ask you to take a quick selfie to confirm it's you.

Your selfie is not stored by Microsoft and is never sent to Hollins.

How Face Check Uses Your Photo

What happens

Microsoft Authenticator takes a short live selfie to confirm a real person is present. It then compares your selfie with the photo in your Hollins credential, which is your Hollins profile photo.

Who does the comparison

Microsoft does, using Azure AI services that are part of Microsoft Entra Verified ID. Hollins does not run any facial recognition.

What is kept

Microsoft says that Authenticator, Verified ID, and the Azure AI services do not store or keep your selfie. Hollins never receives it.

What Hollins receives

A match score showing how closely your selfie matched your photo, plus the details already in your credential, like your name and Hollins email. We keep a record of the score, the time, and whether a pass was issued for security auditing. We don't use it for anything else.

It's your choice

If you'd rather not use Face Check, contact the IT help desk and we'll confirm your identity another way.
